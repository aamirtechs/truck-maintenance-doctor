Truck Maintenance Doctor

Audit a fleet maintenance schedule CSV for the mistakes that put a truck out of service: preventive maintenance past its due date, services due soon, services overdue by miles, missing last-service records, and odometer regression.

Your report will appear here.

Errors: overdue-service, overdue-by-miles, missing-last-service, missing-due, duplicate-service, odometer-regression, missing-unit-id, missing-service-type, invalid-date, future-date, invalid-miles, invalid-status. Warnings: service-due-soon, due-by-miles-soon, unknown-service-type, last-service-after-due. A service due exactly at the window edge is flagged, a service due today is due soon rather than overdue, and a completed service is never flagged as overdue or due soon. Mileages are whole numbers and accept thousands separators.
